# Landing Page Overrides

> **PROJECT:** YAGO
> **Page:** landing
> Rules in this file override `design-system/yago/MASTER.md` when they conflict.

---

## Layout Overrides

- The landing should alternate between `soft` and `strong` visual density to maintain rhythm.
- The hero must remain a two-part composition on desktop: direct message on the left, operational preview on the right.
- On mobile, the copy must stay first and the preview must follow without feeling like a detached second section.

## Hero Rules

- The `h1` should stay short, stacked, and easy to scan in under 3 seconds.
- The subheading should explain the business outcome, not the full technical stack.
- Trust chips under the hero copy should stay compact and credibility-oriented.
- The hero preview should feel like a control surface or operating snapshot, not a fake dashboard.

## CTA Rules

- Primary CTA: booking or contact intent.
- Secondary CTA: exploration intent.
- Tertiary links are allowed only if visually quieter than both CTAs.

## Section Rules

- `Servicios` should emphasize implementation capability and ownership.
- `Productos` should feel more packaged and scalable than `Servicios`.
- `OCR Master` should carry a slightly more premium tone than the rest of the landing.
- `Proceso` must reduce risk and complexity.
- `FAQ` should answer objections in a straightforward tone.

## Mobile Rules

- Preserve a strong top-of-page impression before the first scroll.
- Keep side padding generous enough to avoid a cramped look.
- Reduce ambient effects if they start competing with text clarity.
- Metrics, chips, and cards must remain scannable without horizontal compression.

## Motion Rules

- Above the fold, use motion to direct attention, not to decorate.
- Hero motion should feel calm and premium.
- Scroll reveals should keep a consistent cadence across sections.
- Avoid giving each section a different animation personality.
